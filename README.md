# Hybrid-ai-co2-forecasting
A review of hybrid AI and deep learning models for CO₂ emission forecasting, exploring ARIMA, LSTM, Bayesian networks, and genetic algorithms across transportation, industry, and indoor air quality, with a focus on improving forecasting accuracy and adaptability to climate-related disruptions.
A comprehensive review of hybrid AI and deep learning approaches for enhanced CO₂ emission forecasting. The paper examines the integration of ARIMA/SARIMA, LSTM, Bayesian neural networks, genetic algorithms, and other machine learning techniques to capture complex temporal and nonlinear emission patterns. Applications across transportation, industrial processes, and indoor air quality are explored, including the impact of COVID-19 disruptions. The review also highlights challenges in data quality, computational complexity, uncertainty, and model interpretability, while discussing future directions such as SHAP, edge AI, and quantum computing for more accurate and adaptive climate forecasting.
